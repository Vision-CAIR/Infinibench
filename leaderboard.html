InfiniBench Leaderboard - Comprehensive evaluation across eight video understanding skills. All models utilize subtitles for enhanced performance. Results show Frame Rate configurations: FPV (Frames Per Video), FPS (Frames Per Second), and FPW (Frames Per Window).

Scoring: Grounding skills (accuracy) are measured as percentages, while Reasoning skills (scores) range from 0 to 10. Average Accuracy is calculated from grounding skills, and Average Score is calculated from reasoning skills.

Ranking Methodology: The final ranking score represents a balanced evaluation combining both grounding and reasoning capabilities. It is calculated using a weighted formula that normalizes accuracy percentages (0-100) and reasoning scores (0-10) to equivalent scales:
Ranking Score = (0.5 × Accuracy/100 + 0.5 × Score/10) × 100
This methodology ensures equal contribution from both skill categories, providing a comprehensive assessment of model performance across all video understanding tasks.
